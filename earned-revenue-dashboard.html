Group 1 Pivot Data — Personnel Input Model

Check a box in any Forecast month to add that person to the Personnel-Input Forecast for that month — adds 1 to their department's headcount (C2), which becomes Hours (B2) via the department's own Hours-per-Worker, and recalculates % (A2), all in the table above. Filter by Group or Report, or use a column's ↩ Prior button to carry forward last month's roster. The "Total (Filtered)" row at the bottom of the table sums just these filtered people's own Actual hours and Forecast headcount — it responds to the filter the same way Forecast already does; company-wide Revenue and the department totals above stay unaffected.
