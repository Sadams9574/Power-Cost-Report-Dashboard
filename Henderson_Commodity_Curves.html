Man-hour table by cost code

Every change is saved and shown here. Manpower drives the hours: the curve spreads people per week (whole people by default), hours = FTE × 60, and each code's hours still equal its BL hours. Change a curve, change a Start or Finish date, or type manpower by week in the Manpower rows (the code switches to Manual FTE). Δ vs BL shows how far typed manpower is over (▲) or under (▼) the BL hours, and Fit scales it back. Enter moves down, arrow keys move around, and you can paste a block from Excel. Highlighted dates differ from the original baseline (hover over one to see the BL date). Filter by type or pick cost codes, and the graphs below follow.
